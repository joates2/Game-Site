Chess Rules

Objective: Trap your opponent's King in checkmate so that it cannot escape capture.

Piece Movements:
• Pawn: Moves forward one square (two on first move), captures diagonally.
• Knight: Moves in an L-shape (2 squares one way, 1 square perpendicular) and can jump over pieces.
• Bishop: Moves diagonally any number of open squares.
• Rook: Moves horizontally or vertically any number of open squares.
• Queen: Combines the power of the Rook and Bishop.
• King: Moves one square in any direction.
