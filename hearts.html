How to Play Hearts

Objective: Have the lowest score when any player reaches 100 points.

Card Points: Each Heart (♥) is worth 1 point, and the Queen of Spades (Q♠) is worth 13 points.

Passing: Before each round, select 3 cards to pass to another player (Left, Right, Across, or Hold every 4th round).

Gameplay: The player holding the 2♣ leads the first trick. You must follow the led suit if you can; otherwise, you may play any card. The highest card of the led suit wins the trick.

Breaking Hearts: You cannot lead a Heart until a Heart or the Q♠ has been discarded on a previous trick (unless you only have Hearts left).

Shooting the Moon: If you capture all 26 points in a single round, you score 0 points and every opponent receives 26 points!
