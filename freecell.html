FreeCell Rules

Objective: Move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles, built up by suit from Ace to King (Ace, 2, 3... King).

Free Cells: The four open slots at the top left can temporarily hold any single card.

Tableau Piles: Build descending sequences alternating colors (e.g., a Black 8 on a Red 9). You can move structured sequences depending on available empty free cells and columns.

Empty Columns: Any card or valid descending sequence can be moved into an empty tableau column.

Victory!

You cleared all foundations cleanly.
