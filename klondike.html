How to Play Klondike Solitaire

Objective: Move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles, built up by suit from Ace to King (Ace, 2, 3... King).

Tableau Piles: Build descending sequences alternating colors (e.g., a Black 8 on a Red 9). You can move groups of cards together.

Empty Spaces: Only Kings (or piles starting with a King) can be placed into empty tableau columns.

Stock & Waste: Click the stock pile to draw cards into the waste pile. You can cycle through the stock deck multiple times.

Victory!

Congratulations! You cleared all 52 cards!
