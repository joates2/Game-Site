How to Play Sheepshead

Objective: Reach 61 or more card points as the Picker team to win the hand.

Deck & Trumps: Played with a 32-card deck (7 through Ace). Queens are highest trumps, followed by Jacks, followed by Diamonds.

Picking Phase: Players have the option to pick the 2-card blind. If picked, the picker takes the blind into their hand and buries 2 cards.

Gameplay: Players must follow the led type (Trump vs Fail suit) if able.
