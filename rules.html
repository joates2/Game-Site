Klondike Solitaire

Play Now

Rules & Objective

The goal is to move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles, built up by suit from Ace to King. Tableau columns are built down in alternating colors (red/black). Unlocked face-down cards are flipped face up.

Basic Strategy

Expose face-down cards as quickly as possible. Avoid emptying a tableau column unless you have a King ready to fill it, as Kings are required to start new column piles.

FreeCell

Play Now

Rules & Objective

All 52 cards are dealt face-up into 8 tableau columns. Use four temporary open cells to hold single cards out of the way. Build tableaus down in alternating colors and foundations up by suit from Ace to King.

Basic Strategy

Keep as many free cells empty as possible; every occupied cell halves the number of cards you can dynamically move in sequence. Clear Aces and low cards to foundations early.

Spider Solitaire

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Played with two decks (104 cards). Build descending sequences from King down to Ace within the 10 columns. When a complete suited run is formed, it automatically flies off to the foundation.

Basic Strategy

Try to build sequences within the same suit whenever possible. Mixing suits locks cards in place until lower cards are cleared away.

Chess

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Two players battle across a 64-square board with unique piece movements. The objective is checkmate—trapping the opponent's King so it cannot escape capture.

Basic Strategy

Control the center squares early with pawns and minor pieces (knights and bishops). Castle early to keep your King safe and activate your rooks.

Checkers

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Pieces move diagonally forward on dark squares. Jump over opponent pieces to capture them. Reach the furthest row to crown your piece into a King, which can move and jump backwards.

Basic Strategy

Keep your back row intact as long as possible to block enemy pieces from becoming Kings. Control the center diagonals and force your opponent into unfavorable trades.

Mexican Train Dominoes

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Players build domino trains outward from a central engine hub. If you cannot play on your turn, you must draw; if you still cannot play, your personal train becomes open for others. Lowest total pips after multiple rounds wins.

Basic Strategy

Try to dump high-value double tiles early so you aren't stuck holding heavy points if someone else goes out quickly. Pay attention to what numbers your opponents are missing.

Dominoes

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Match open pip ends on the layout. Be the first player to empty your hand of tiles or score the most points by hitting multiples of 5 on open ends.

Basic Strategy

Keep a balanced variety of suits in your hand. If you hold multiple tiles of the same number, try to force the board to open on that number to trap your opponents.

Cribbage

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Peg your way across the board to 121 points. Score points during play (summing to 15, pairs, runs) and during hand counting, including a special dealer's box called the crib.

Basic Strategy

Be mindful of what you throw into the crib. If you are the dealer, load it with scoring potential (5s, matching pairs); if opponent is dealer, toss "spacer" cards like 4s and 7s or low connectors.

Crazy Eights

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Match the rank or suit of the top discard pile card. Eights are wild and can be played on any turn to call a new active suit. First player to empty their hand wins.

Basic Strategy

Hold onto your Eights until late in the game when you get stuck or need to escape a dangerous suit trap set by opponents.

Go Fish

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Ask opponents for specific card ranks to complete sets of four (books). If they don't have it, they tell you to "Go Fish" from the pond. Most books wins.

Basic Strategy

Keep track of what cards your opponents request; if someone asks you for a rank, remember that they likely hold matching cards in their hand.

Old Maid

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Discard all pairs from your starting hand. Take turns drawing one card blindly from the player to your left. Avoid getting stuck holding the unmatchable Queen (the Old Maid).

Basic Strategy

If you hold the Queen, try to position it cleanly in your fan so opponents accidentally pick it. Pay attention to card positions when an opponent draws from you.

Rummy 500

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Form melds (sets of 3-4 of a kind, or suited runs of 3+). Score points for cards laid down on the table. First player to reach 500 points wins.

Basic Strategy

You can pick up multiple cards from the discard pile as long as you immediately meld the bottom card. This is high risk/reward for staging massive scoring turns.

Hand and Foot

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Multi-deck rummy variant where each player gets a "Hand" and a "Foot" stack of cards. Play out your Hand, then pick up your Foot to fulfill book requirements before going out.

Basic Strategy

Coordinate book building with your partner. Prioritize forming clean red canastas (no wild cards) as they award massive bonus points at round end.

Canasta

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Build "Canastas"—melds of 7 or more cards of the same rank. You must complete at least one Canasta before you are allowed to go out and end the round.

Basic Strategy

Freezing the discard pile by discarding a wild card prevents opponents from easily sweeping up massive stacks of cards.

Gin Rummy

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Form your cards into sets and runs. Reduce your unmatched "deadwood" card points below 10 to knock, or eliminate deadwood entirely for a "Gin" bonus.

Basic Strategy

Hold onto middle cards (7s, 8s) early on as they can connect in runs in multiple directions. Watch discard patterns to deduce what your opponent is holding.

Rummy

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Draw and discard cards on each turn to build sets of 3-4 matching ranks or consecutive suited runs. Empty your hand or lay down valid melds to win.

Basic Strategy

Don't hold onto high-value face cards too long if they aren't forming a meld, as they incur heavy point penalties if someone else goes out first.

Hearts

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Avoid taking tricks containing penalty Hearts (1 point each) and the Queen of Spades (13 points). Lowest score wins—unless you "Shoot the Moon" by capturing all scoring cards.

Basic Strategy

Unload high spades early or hold the Ace/King of Spades to protect yourself from being forced to take the Queen of Spades.

Spades

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Bid how many tricks you and your partner will win. Spades are permanent trump. Fulfill your combined bid while avoiding accumulated overtrick "sandbags."

Basic Strategy

Count your "sure tricks" (high cards in non-trump suits and high spades) before locking in your bid. Avoid taking unnecessary extra tricks once your team bid is met.

Euchre

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Played with a compact 24-card deck (9 through Ace). Partnership trick-taking where the Jack of the trump suit (Right Bower) and the other Jack of the same color (Left Bower) are the highest trumps.

Basic Strategy

If you order up your partner or call trump, make sure you hold at least three winning tricks or a Bower-Ace combo to secure your maker quota.

Pinochle

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Uses a 48-card specialized double deck (9 down to Ace in 2 copies per suit). Game consists of a meld-scoring phase followed by high-intensity trick-taking.

Basic Strategy

Memorize standard meld combinations (like the Queen of Spades + Jack of Diamonds for a Pinochle). Bid aggressively if your hand holds multiple marriages or runs.

Bridge

Play Now

Rules & Objective

The premier 4-player partnership game. Involves an auction bidding phase to determine contract level and trump suit, followed by declarer play where partner's hand is laid face up as dummy.

Basic Strategy

Count high card points (HCP) accurately during bidding. During play, establish long suits in dummy and preserve control cards (like Aces) to cut enemy momentum.

Whist

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Pure trick-taking without bidding. Every trick won above six tricks scores 1 point for your partnership team toward the winning match target.

Basic Strategy

Pay strict attention to lead cards. Signal your partner by leading high cards from strong sequences or low cards to encourage partner leads.

Sheepshead

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Classic 32-card point-trick game. Queens, Jacks, and all Diamonds are permanent trumps. Collect card points (Aces = 11, 10s = 10, Kings = 4, etc.) to exceed 61 points.

Basic Strategy

If you pick the blind, discard deadwood points safely. As a defender, trap the picker by leaking low cards and withholding your high trump power until crucial tricks.

Sergeant Major

Play Now

Rules & Objective

Three-player trick-taking game (3-5-8 format). Players have individual quotas of tricks to capture (dealer takes 3, second takes 5, third takes 8). Surplus cards are swapped between players.

Basic Strategy

Evaluate your hand carefully during card swaps. Pass low or useless cards to opponents while keeping high trumps to secure your exact individual quota.
