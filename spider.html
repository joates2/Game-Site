Spider Solitaire Rules

Objective: Assemble 8 complete descending sequences from King down to Ace in the tableau. Once a full suit run is formed, it is automatically removed to the foundation.

Tableau Building: Build descending sequences down regardless of suit (e.g., any 8 on any 9). However, only cards of the same suit in sequential order can be moved together as a group.

Empty Columns: Any single card or valid same-suit sequence can be placed into an empty tableau column.

Stock Pile: Click the stock deck at any time to deal one new face-up card to every tableau column (note: no column can be empty when dealing).
