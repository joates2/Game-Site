Rummy Rules

Objective: Form valid sets and runs with your cards and be the first player to empty your hand (go out).

Melds: Valid melds require at least 3 cards and can be either Sets (same rank, different suits) or Runs (consecutive cards of the same suit).

Turns: On your turn, draw a card from either the stock pile or discard pile, optionally lay down melds, and finish by discarding one card.
