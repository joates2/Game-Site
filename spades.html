How to Play Spades

Objective: Be the first team to reach 500 points in this partnership game (You & Partner vs. Bot 1 & Bot 3).

Bidding: At the start of each round, players bid how many tricks they expect to win. Partnership bids combine together.

Gameplay: Players must follow the led suit if able. If you cannot follow suit, you may play any card, including a Spade (♠).

Trumps & Breaking Spades: Spades are permanently trump. You cannot lead a Spade until Spades have been "broken" or you have only Spades left.

Scoring: Making your bid awards 10 points per trick bid, plus 1 point for extra tricks (sandbags). Failing to meet your combined bid incurs a penalty.
