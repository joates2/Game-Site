Hand and Foot Rules

Objective: Form melds and books of cards with your partner, play through both your Hand and your Foot, and score the most points.

Minimum Initial Melds: Your team's first meld of a round must meet minimum point requirements based on your team's total score (50 pts for 0 score, 90 pts for 1-1495, 120 pts for 1500-2995, and 150 pts for 3000+).

Books: A book requires 7 or more cards of the same rank. Clean Books (no wild cards) score +500 points. Dirty Books (containing wild cards like 2s or Jokers) score +300 points.

Card Point Values: Jokers = 50 pts; Aces & 2s = 20 pts; 8 through King = 10 pts; 4 through 7 = 5 pts.
