Rummy 500 Rules

Objective: Be the first player to reach 500 points across rounds by forming melds (sets and runs) and laying off cards.

Melds: Valid melds require at least 3 cards and can be either Sets (same rank, different suits) or Runs (consecutive cards of the same suit).

Picking Up Discards: You can pick up multiple cards from the discard pile, but you must immediately use the bottom-most picked card in a valid meld or layoff during your turn.

Scoring: Melds on the table score positive points (Aces are 15, Face cards/10s are 10, others face value). Unmelded cards left in your hand at the end of a round are deducted from your score.
