Dominoes Draw Game Rules

Setup & Tiles:
• 2 Players: Each player is dealt 7 tiles.
• 3 or 4 Players: Each player is dealt 5 tiles.
• Remaining tiles are placed face down in the boneyard.

First Move: The player holding the highest double plays first. If no player holds a double, the player with the heaviest (highest pip total) tile starts.

Gameplay & Turns:
• Play proceeds clockwise around the table.
• Players match a tile's end number to either open end of the domino train on the board.
• You can drag and drop tiles onto the board or click a tile in your hand then click the closest open end on the board.

Drawing from Boneyard: If you cannot make a valid move on your turn, you must draw tiles from the boneyard until you find a playable tile or the boneyard is empty.

Winning the Round: The first player to successfully empty their hand wins the round!
