Dominoes Rules

Objective: Be the first player to empty your hand by matching tile end numbers to the open ends on the board.

Gameplay:
• Select a tile from your hand, then click either Play Left End or Play Right End.
• The matching number must equal the open end of the layout.

Drawing: If you have no playable tiles, you must draw from the boneyard until you can play or the boneyard is empty.
