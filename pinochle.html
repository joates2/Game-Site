How to Play Pinochle

Objective: First team to reach 1,000 points wins the game.

Deck & Cards: Played with a 48-card Pinochle deck (two of each 9, 10, J, Q, K, A). Card values: Ace = 11, Ten = 10, King = 4, Queen = 3, Jack = 2, Nine = 0.

Bidding: Teams bid on the minimum card points they expect to capture in tricks.

Gameplay: 12 cards are dealt to each player. Players take turns playing tricks following suit and trump rules.
