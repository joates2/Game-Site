Crazy Eights Rules

Objective: Be the first player to get rid of all the cards in your hand.

Matching: On your turn, you must play a card that matches either the suit or the rank of the top card on the discard pile.

Eights are Wild: Eights can be played on top of any card at any time. When you play an 8, you get to choose a new active suit for the next player.

Drawing: If you do not have a valid card to play, you must draw from the stock pile until you can play or the stock runs out.
