Checkers Rules

Objective: Capture all of your opponent's pieces or block them so they have no legal moves remaining.

Movement & Captures:
• Pieces move diagonally forward one square at a time on dark squares.
• Jump over an opponent piece into an empty square behind it to capture. Multi-jumps are mandatory when available!

King Promotion: When a piece reaches the furthest row on the opponent's side, it becomes a King, allowing it to move and capture both forward and backward.
