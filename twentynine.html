How to Play Twenty-Nine

Objective: Win tricks containing high-value cards to meet or exceed your team's bid (16 to 28 points). Total points in the deck equal 29 (28 card points + 1 point for the last trick).

Card Ranking & Points: Played with 32 cards (7 through Ace). In every suit, the ranking from highest to lowest is: J (3 pts), 9 (2 pts), A (1 pt), 10 (1 pt), followed by K, Q, 8, and 7 (0 pts each).

Bidding & Trump: Players inspect their first 4 cards to place a bid. The winning bidder selects the hidden trump suit, and the remaining 4 cards are dealt to each player.

Gameplay: You must follow the suit led if you are able. If you have none of the led suit, you may play a trump card or discard.
