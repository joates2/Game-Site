Mexican Train Rules

Objective: Be the first player to empty your hand of all domino tiles by playing them onto valid train tracks.

The Engine: The game starts from a central double tile (the Engine, e.g., Double-12).

Valid Plays:
• You can play on your own personal train.
• You can play on the shared Mexican Train at any time.
• You can play on an opponent's train only if their train marker is up (marked as Open).

Drawing: If you cannot make a valid move from your hand, you must draw a tile. If you still cannot play, your train becomes marked open for others, and your turn ends.
