How to Play Mexican Train

Stacked Horizontal Layout: The Engine hub anchors the top-left corner, and all train tracks extend horizontally from left to right, stacked vertically on top of each other.

Full Game (13 Rounds): The game spans 13 rounds, starting with the Double-12 engine and stepping down each round to Double-0.

Controls (Click or Drag & Drop): Click a tile in your hand to select it and click anywhere on the train row you want, or simply drag and drop any tile directly onto a train track row.

Taking Your Turn:
• First Turn Only: On your very first turn of a round, you can play as many matching tiles as you want onto your personal train.
• Later Turns: After your opening turn, you are restricted to playing just one tile per turn.

Stuck & Drawing: If you cannot make a legal move, you must draw a tile from the boneyard. If it fits, you can play it right away. If it still doesn't fit, your train marker goes up (making your train Open to others), and your turn ends.

Playing Doubles: Whenever a double tile is played, it creates a special condition: either the player who laid it or the next person must immediately play a tile to satisfy (resolve) the double before doing anything else.

Scoring: Unplayed tiles at the end of each round count as penalty points (sum of their pips). The player with the lowest total score after all 13 rounds wins.
