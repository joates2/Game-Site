How to Play Bridge

Objective: Win tricks according to your agreed partnership contract (Level + 6 tricks required to make).

Bidding: Choose your contract level (1-7) and strain (Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs, or No Trump).

Gameplay: 13 cards are dealt to each player. Partner becomes your exposed dummy partner after the auction. You must follow the led suit whenever able.
