How to Play Euchre

Objective: First team to score 10 points wins the game.

Bidding & Trump: Players choose whether to make the turned-up card suit trump (Round 1) or choose a new suit (Round 2). The Jack of trump is the Right Bower, and the Jack of the same color is the Left Bower (also counted as trump).

Gameplay: 5 cards are dealt to each player. You must follow the led suit if able. Trump cards beat any non-trump card.

Scoring: The maker team must win at least 3 tricks to score points (1 point for 3-4 tricks, 2 points for a 5-trick sweep). Winning all 5 tricks as a lone player scores 4 points. If the makers fail to win 3 tricks, they are "Euchred" and the defending team gets 2 points.
