Go Fish Rules

Setup & Deal: 7 cards are dealt to each player for 2–3 players, and 5 cards for 4+ players. Remaining cards form the central Fish Pond stock.

Asking for Cards: On your turn, pick an opponent and ask them for a specific rank. Rule: You must hold at least one card of that requested rank in your own hand.

If They Have It: They must hand over all cards of that rank, and you take another turn.

If They Say "Go Fish!": Draw one card from the stock pond. If you draw the exact card rank you asked for, show it and take another turn! Otherwise, your turn ends.

Laying Down Books: Collect all 4 cards of the same rank to immediately score a four-of-a-kind book. If your hand becomes empty, you draw new cards from the stock.

Winning: The game ends when all 13 books are won. The player with the most books wins!
