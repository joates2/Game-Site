How to Play Whist

Objective: Win tricks to score points and be the first team/player to reach the target score (5 points for 4-player, 7 points for 2-player)[cite: 16].

Gameplay: Each player is dealt 13 cards[cite: 16]. The last card of the deck is turned up to determine the trump suit.

Following Suit: You must follow the suit led if you are able. If you don't have cards of the led suit, you may play any card (including trump cards)[cite: 16].

Scoring: In 4-player partnership mode, every trick won over 6 tricks counts as 1 point for your team[cite: 16].
