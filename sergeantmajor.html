How to Play Sergeant Major

Objective: Win more tricks than your assigned target to gain positive points (and force opponents below their targets).

Deck & Deal: Played with a standard 52-card deck for 3 players. Each player receives 16 cards (with 4 cards set aside in the widow). Spades are trump.

Targets & Scoring: At the end of each 16-trick hand, your score changes by the difference between the tricks you won and your target (e.g., winning 7 tricks on a target of 5 gives +2 points).

Gameplay: You must follow the suit led if you are able. If you cannot follow suit, you may play a trump card (Spades) or discard.
