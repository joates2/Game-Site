Cribbage Quick Rules

  • 1. The Crib: Both players are dealt 6 cards and discard 2 into the dealer's bonus hand (the Crib).
  • 2. Pegging (Up to 31): Alternate playing cards without exceeding 31. Score 2 pts for hitting 15 or 31, 2 pts for pairs (6 for 3-of-a-kind), 1 pt/card for runs of 3+, and 1 pt for "Go" / last card.
  • 3. His Heels: If the cut Starter card is a Jack, the dealer immediately pegs 2 points.
  • 4. The Show: Count your 4 hand cards + the Starter card for 15s (2 pts each), pairs (2 pts), runs (1 pt/card), flushes (4-5 pts), and His Nobs (Jack of starter suit = 1 pt).
