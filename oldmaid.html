Old Maid Rules

Objective: Form pairs and discard them from your hand. Avoid being left holding the Old Maid (Queen of Spades) at the end of the game.

Setup: The Queen of Clubs is removed from the deck so the Queen of Spades has no matching pair (becoming the unmatched Old Maid).

Gameplay: On your turn, click a face-down card from an opponent's hand to draw it. If it matches a card in your hand, the pair is automatically discarded.

Winning: The first player to clear all cards from their hand wins. The player left holding the Old Maid loses!

You Won!

You paired all your cards and avoided the Old Maid.
